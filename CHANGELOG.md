# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Announcements now sits in the Simplify group of Pelican's admin menu, together with the other Simplify add-ons,
  in the same order everywhere. This works with or without the Simplify core.

## 1.0.0

- Announcements as Info, Warning or Success bars at the top of every page, from a start time until an end time.
- "Also put in everyone's notifications" puts an announcement in the bell of everyone who sees it, until it ends.
- "Also send as app notification" sends it through Simplify's app notifications, when Simplify has them turned on.
- "Also send in game" posts it in the chat of running game servers, with a chat command per game.
- Everyone can unpin a bar. It then waits in the bell with a button to pin it again.
- Shows up in Simplify's add-on list when Simplify is installed.
